Ivie

Mobile developer at Holland & Barrett, 2021 - 2022

Ivie was a personalized wellness app powered by Holland & Barrett: order a blood test, get results in 48 hours reviewed by a doctor, and book a consultation with an expert. Built in Flutter. The app has since been discontinued.

Screenshots from the archived App Store listing.

The product

Users set their goals, register a home blood test kit and see the results in the app within 48 hours. Each biomarker shows where it falls in the reference range, along with a personal review from a doctor. From there they can get free advice from an advisor or book a paid GP video consultation.

My time at Holland & Barrett

When Holland & Barrett acquired Beyond100, I moved over and continued work on its health research app, H&B&Me, then served as mobile lead on a new project. I designed a modular architecture that let features be reused across H&B's apps instead of being rewritten, and built custom chart widgets with complex animations.
