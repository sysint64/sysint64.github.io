Finblox

Mobile developer, 2022

Finblox was a crypto platform where users bought, swapped and earned yield on their assets, with weekly prize draws and its own FBX token. I built core trading and growth features in its Flutter app. The app has since been discontinued.

Screenshots from the archived Google Play listing.

What I built

The coin swap feature and peer-to-peer order creation, and the launchpad buy-token screen used for the FBX token sale. For onboarding, I integrated token purchases through Meld, QR code sign-in and deep linking.

I also built the insights tabs, redesigned the settings page, contributed to Pool Party, the weekly prize draw, improved pagination across the app and wrote unit, widget, golden and integration tests.

Stack

Flutter, Dart.
