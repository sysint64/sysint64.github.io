TTM Wallet

Lead mobile developer

TTM Wallet is a non-custodial, multi-currency crypto wallet for iOS and Android. I led its mobile development in Flutter. The app was later redesigned and lives on as MDAO Wallet.

  • Google Play (MDAO Wallet)

Screenshots from the original Russian App Store listing, before the redesign.

The product

The private key is generated and stored on the device and never leaves it; access is protected by biometrics, a PIN code or a password. The wallet holds BTC, ETH, ERC-20 tokens, TRX and other assets, with balances, history and price charts, quick exchange between currencies and access to decentralized apps.
