Andrei Kabylin

Lead mobile engineer

I've been building iOS and Android apps for over a decade, mostly in Flutter, with Swift and Kotlin where native code matters. Most recently I built BookWalker from scratch: a manga, light novel and audiobook app with its own comic reader engine, an EPUB reader on native Readium, offline mode and DRM, packaged as modules other apps can reuse.

Before that I led mobile at Comikey, rebuilding its reader and making purchases reliable, and worked on apps for Finblox, Holland & Barrett and TTM Wallet. I care about architecture and tests as much as features, and contribute to Flutter and other open-source projects.
